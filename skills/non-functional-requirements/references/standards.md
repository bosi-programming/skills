# Non-functional Requirements Standards

## Be specific and measurable

Avoid terms like "the software must be fast". Use instead terms like "the software needs to answer in less than 2 seconds for 95% of the requests".

## Use known quality patterns

Do not create rules out of nowhere. Use known quality patterns like ISO/IEC 25002:2024, ISO/IEC 25010:2023 and ISO/IEC 25019:2023 to define the NFRs.

## Prioritize the NFRs

Different features and products will give different priorities to their NFRs. Rank them with MoSCoW (Must, Should, Could, Won't).

## Consider the architectural impact

NFRs can impact the architectural decisions and it should be evaluated during definition.

## Create clear acceptance criteria

Use terms like "the software must be up 99.99% of the time each month", instead of "the software must be up almost every second".

## Link the NFRs to business rules and user stories

NFRs must be linked to other functionalities or flows.
